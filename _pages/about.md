I am a Ph.D. candidate in Information and Communication Engineering at the Future Technology Institute, South China University of Technology (SCUT), and expect to graduate in 2027. I am also a visiting Ph.D. researcher at the AAU Energy, Aalborg University. Before beginning my doctoral studies, I worked as a technical engineer at Shenzhen Metro, focusing on train main circuits, auxiliary inverter systems, vehicle assembly, and commissioning.

My research lies at the intersection of power electronics, artificial intelligence, and complex engineering systems. I am particularly interested in enabling critical equipment to achieve more reliable condition awareness, fault diagnosis, and autonomous decision-making.

Current Work
======

- Leading intelligent UAV inspection and industrial internet projects for China Southern Power Grid.
- Designing and advancing the engineering implementation of an AI-powered security inspection robot system for Shenzhen Metro.
- Investigating open-circuit fault diagnosis, virtual sample generation, and edge-cloud collaborative AIoT for T-type three-level inverters.
- Contributing to generative-AI construction planning, dual-spectrum robotic inspection, and wheeled-legged robotic inspection.

Selected Highlights
======

- First author of six SCI journal papers, with work published in *IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement*, *IEEE Internet of Things Journal*, and *IET Electric Power Applications*.
- Inventor of one granted patent, with additional patent applications in robotics, intelligent security inspection, and point-cloud processing.
- Led a multi-brand contactor adaptation project for metro trains that reduced maintenance costs by approximately 50%, saving about RMB 14.62 million.
- The AI-powered security inspection robot project received the Innovation Technology Presentation Award from the Shenzhen Rail Transit Association.
